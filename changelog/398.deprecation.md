Passing `skipna=False` together with `skipna_evaluation_dims` to `da.pr.downscale_timeseries`, `ds.pr.downscale_timeseries`, or `ds.pr.downscale_gas_timeseries` is deprecated and emits a `DeprecationWarning`. Pass `skipna_evaluation_dims` alone instead, like in all other functions. In a future release, passing both will raise a `ValueError`, like it already does in `pr.sum`.
