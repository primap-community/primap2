Processing information now stores only the last `primap2.ProcessingStepDescription` of
each timeseries, which refers to earlier steps via `parents` and records the `variable`
and `coords` of its timeseries; step times are `primap2.TimeRange`s.
`primap2.TimeseriesProcessingDescription` and `time="all"` were removed, and files saved
with earlier versions can no longer be read.
